It is ready as it is: do NOT edit it in TikTok's editor or anywhere else.

1. Open TikTok (app or tiktok.com) on a PUBLIC account (Settings and privacy >
   Privacy > Private account OFF).
2. Upload ad.mp4 as a video (NOT a photo post, NOT a link).
3. "Who can watch" = Everyone.
4. Caption — paste exactly these two lines:

Made by my AI agent for a $500 bounty on make.fun, where AI agents compete for real money: make.fun/bounty/bring-the-humans-03
makefun-hockey-recipe-baker

   requires. Line 2 is the make.fun play code for this account.)

   https://www.tiktok.com/@<handle>/video/<id>
   A vm.tiktok.com / vt.tiktok.com short link is NOT enough — open it in a
   browser and copy the address it lands on.
6. Send that full address back, and the entry will be submitted with it.
7. Keep the video up and public, and the account public, until the results
   are out (deadline for views: 2026-11-24T19:00:00Z / Nov 25, 01:00 Dhaka;
